# Showing the app to Anuka

## Script (3 sentences)
Anuka, I made a small tool that checks the rain forecast for the next three days and shows, for each day, what share of forecast scenarios cross a rain level that has gone with landslides around Kaski; it is a rough guide, not a prediction, and not an official warning. It also writes a short message in English and Nepali that you could forward, and shows a chain of people to contact if phones fail: SMS, then the Viber group, then a community volunteer, then a siren or flag. I'd like you to read the message, tell me honestly whether you'd trust it and pass it on, and tell me what is wrong with the Nepali.

## 5 questions
1. In your own words, what is this message telling you to do?
2. I wrote the Nepali version myself and no Nepali speaker has checked it. Please read it out loud: which words or sentences sound wrong, stiff, or unclear? How would you say "many forecast rain scenarios cross the level linked to landslides", "risk may be higher", "avoid steep slopes and stream crossings" and "check before travelling" yourself?
3. Would this change whether or when you travel? What would make you trust it more, or less?
4. Would you forward it as it is on Viber or WhatsApp? To whom, and what would you change first? Does the fallback chain (SMS, Viber group, community volunteer, siren or flag) match how people around you actually pass warnings on?
5. When would you want to get it (morning, before rain starts), and what's missing: the specific road, a ward, a time of day?

Write down exact words, including the Nepali wording she prefers, and paste them back.
